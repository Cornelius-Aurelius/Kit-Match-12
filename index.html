How to play

01

Study Number 12

Look carefully at the reference kit. Inspect shirt patterns, badge symbols, sleeve details, shorts markings and sock bands.

02

Choose the exact match

Select a teammate with the identical kit. The shirt number is different on purpose; you are matching the kit, not the number.

03

Build your streak

Correct passes earn points and advance automatically. Wrong choices explain the difference. Campaign has three lives; Practice is untimed.

Why these cues? Colour alone isn't a reliable signal for everyone. Patterns, symbols and clothing details provide extra information. This is an educational football game, not a medical test or a simulation of colour vision deficiency. Read The FA's colour-blindness guidance.
